4 Payments and pricing

4.1 Fare calculation

Fares are calculated based on base rate, distance, time, and current demand. When you book a ride, you will see an upfront fixed price before confirming. This is the amount you will be charged unless the trip changes significantly (extra stops, route changes requested by you, etc.).

4.2 Payment methods

We accept major credit and debit cards, as well as our in-app wallet. By adding a payment method, you authorize us to charge it for rides, tips, cancellation fees, and any other amounts you owe.

4.3 Tips

100% of tips go directly to the driver. Tips are optional and can be added before or after a trip.

4.4 Cancellation fees

You can cancel free of charge within 2 minutes of booking. After that, a small fee may apply to compensate the driver for their time and fuel.

4.5 Refunds

If something goes wrong on a ride — incorrect fare, driver issue, or unexpected charge — you can request a refund within 30 days from My Trips. Most refund requests are reviewed within 24 hours.
