About InstantCab India

We're making Indian cities move better.

InstantCab is on a mission to make urban mobility safe, affordable, and sustainable across India — for riders, drivers, and the cities we serve.

Our mission

Faster rides. Fairer pay. A cleaner India.

Since 2022, InstantCab has been connecting millions of riders with professional drivers across 50+ Indian cities. We built our platform on a simple idea: treat drivers like partners, not contractors — and everyone wins.

By giving drivers 80% of every fare and investing in an all-electric fleet by 2030, we're proving that profit and purpose can coexist. That's what better urban mobility looks like.

What we believe

Our values

Riders first

Every feature, every policy, every decision starts with the question: does this make the ride better for the person in the back seat?

Fair to drivers

We pay our drivers more than the industry average and give them real flexibility. A great ride requires a great driver.

Better cities

Fewer empty cars, more shared rides, and an all-electric fleet by 2030. Profit and planet aren't opposites.

Safety without compromise

Real-time tracking, background-checked drivers, and a 24/7 safety team. Never ship a feature that trades safety for speed.

Small teams, big impact

We organise in small autonomous pods that ship weekly. No bloated teams, no endless meetings.

India-first, global-ready

Every product decision considers our 50+ Indian cities. Localisation isn't an afterthought — it's the starting point.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to the questions we hear most.

Your fare is calculated based on a combination of factors: base fare, distance traveled, time spent in the vehicle, and current demand. When you book, you'll see an upfront fixed price in ₹ before you confirm — that's the amount you'll pay, even if the route changes slightly.
Yes — you can schedule a ride up to 30 days in advance from the dashboard. Scheduled rides are confirmed 24 hours before pickup, and your driver is assigned 15 minutes before your scheduled time.
You can cancel free of charge within 2 minutes of booking. After a driver has been assigned and 2 minutes have passed, a small cancellation fee may apply — usually around ₹50.
Your card is charged after the ride is completed, not when you book. You'll receive an email receipt with the final fare within a few minutes of the trip ending.
If something went wrong on a ride, you can request a refund directly from My Trips within 30 days. Most refund requests are reviewed within 24 hours.
Open the trip in My Trips, tap "Contact driver", and let them know what you left. Most drivers respond within an hour. Items are typically held for 30 days.
Safety first, always

Every ride, backed by safety.

From background-checked drivers to real-time tracking and 24/7 emergency support — safety isn't a feature at InstantCab. It's the foundation.

Core safety features

Protection at every step

Background-checked drivers

Every driver passes a comprehensive background check — criminal history, driving record, and identity verification. Re-checked annually.

Real-time GPS tracking

See your driver's exact location, vehicle details, and ETA from the moment they accept your ride. Trip data is logged securely.

Share my trip

Send a live tracking link to a friend or family member with one tap. They'll see your route and ETA in real time.

In-app emergency button

Direct 112 (India's emergency number) access from the app, with your live location and trip details sent to dispatchers automatically.

Ride PIN verification

Confirm your driver with a 4-digit PIN before entering the vehicle. Prevents wrong-car pickups at busy locations.

24/7 safety team

A dedicated safety response team is available around the clock in every market, with 2-minute average response time.
