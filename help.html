Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to the questions we hear most.

Your fare is calculated based on a combination of factors: base fare, distance traveled, time spent in the vehicle, and current demand. When you book, you'll see an upfront fixed price before you confirm — that's the amount you'll pay, even if the route changes slightly.

You can always view the full breakdown of a ride in My Trips.

Yes — you can schedule a ride up to 30 days in advance from the dashboard. Scheduled rides are confirmed 24 hours before pickup, and your driver is assigned 15 minutes before your scheduled time.

Scheduling is especially useful for airport transfers and early morning commutes.

You can cancel free of charge within 2 minutes of booking. After a driver has been assigned and 2 minutes have passed, a small cancellation fee may apply — usually around $5.

If the driver is significantly delayed or can't reach you, the fee is automatically waived.

Your card is charged after the ride is completed, not when you book. You'll receive an email receipt with the final fare within a few minutes of the trip ending.

If you use InstantCab Wallet, the balance is deducted at the same time. You can view all charges in Wallet & Payment Methods.

If something went wrong on a ride — a longer route, driver issue, or incorrect fare — you can request a refund directly from My Trips within 30 days.

Select the trip, tap "Request refund", and tell us what happened. Most refund requests are reviewed within 24 hours.

Every ride includes real-time GPS tracking, driver background checks, in-app emergency calling (911), and a "Share my trip" link you can send to a friend or family member.

You can also verify your driver with the PIN code shown in the app before entering the vehicle.

Open the trip in My Trips, tap "Contact driver", and let them know what you left. Most drivers respond within an hour.

If you can't reach the driver, contact support and we'll help coordinate the return. Items are typically held for 30 days.
