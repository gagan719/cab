Quick answers

Before you reach out

Most questions are answered here. Save yourself the wait.

Open the trip in My Trips, tap "Request refund", and tell us what happened. Most refund requests are reviewed within 24 hours. You have up to 30 days from the ride date to submit one.
Open the trip in My Trips, tap "Contact driver", and let them know what you left. Most drivers respond within an hour. Items are typically held for 30 days.
Visit our driver application page to apply. Requirements: 21+, valid license, a 4-door vehicle from 2010 or newer, and a clean background check. Approval typically takes 3–5 business days.
Head to our InstantCab for Business page to learn about pricing and features, then contact our sales team. We offer centralized billing, travel policies, and expense integrations.
If you're in an emergency, call 911 immediately. For non-urgent concerns, visit our Safety page to file a report. All reports are confidential and reviewed within 24 hours.
