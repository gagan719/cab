Core safety features

Protection at every step

Six layers of safety, built into every ride — whether you're going two blocks or 20 miles.

Background-checked drivers

Every driver passes a comprehensive background check — criminal history, driving record, and identity verification. Re-checked annually.

Real-time GPS tracking

See your driver's exact location, vehicle details, and ETA from the moment they accept your ride. Trip data is logged securely.

Share my trip

Send a live tracking link to a friend or family member with one tap. They'll see your route and ETA in real time.

In-app emergency button

Direct 911 access from the app, with your live location and trip details sent to dispatchers automatically.

Ride PIN verification

Confirm your driver with a 4-digit PIN before entering the vehicle. Prevents wrong-car pickups at busy locations.

24/7 safety team

A dedicated safety response team is available around the clock in every market, with 2-minute average response time.

In an emergency, we're right there with you.

Our in-app emergency button connects you directly to local authorities and our safety team simultaneously — with your live location, driver info, and vehicle details transmitted instantly.

Additional protections

Every detail, covered

Vehicle inspections

All vehicles must pass an annual safety inspection covering brakes, tires, lights, and airbags. Failed inspections mean no driving.

Two-way ratings

Riders rate drivers and drivers rate riders. Low ratings trigger review, and consistently low-rated accounts are removed.

Insurance on every ride

Commercial liability insurance covers every trip from pickup to drop-off — up to $1M per incident.

Block & report

Block any driver or rider from being matched with you again. Reports are investigated within 24 hours.

Anonymized phone numbers

Calls and texts between riders and drivers use masked numbers. Neither party sees the other's real phone number.

Driver training program

Every driver completes our safety training on defensive driving, de-escalation, and emergency procedures before their first ride.

Your safety routine

Four steps to a safer ride

Small habits that make a big difference — every time you ride.

1

Match the details

Before you get in, verify the license plate, car model, and driver photo in the app match the vehicle in front of you.

2

Share your trip

Tap "Share trip" and send the live link to a trusted contact. Takes two seconds.

3

Confirm with PIN

Enter your 4-digit ride PIN when prompted. Your driver will only start the trip after verification.

4

Rate and report

Rate your ride honestly. Report any safety concern — it goes directly to our safety team, not to the driver.
