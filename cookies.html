1 What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that websites and apps place on your device to store information about your visit. They help us remember you, keep you signed in, and understand how the app is used.

We also use similar technologies such as local storage, pixels, and software development kits (SDKs) in our mobile apps. For simplicity, we refer to all of these as "cookies" in this policy.

Cookies don't run programs. They can't carry viruses or access files on your device. They're just small pieces of data that make the app work better.

2 Types of cookies we use

2.1 Strictly necessary (always on)

These cookies are required for the app to function. They handle things like signing you in, keeping your session secure, and processing payments. You cannot disable them.

Cookie Purpose Duration
ic_session Maintains your login session 30 days
ic_csrf Prevents cross-site request forgery attacks Session
ic_payment Keeps your payment flow secure Session

2.2 Functional (recommended)

These cookies remember your preferences and settings to give you a smoother experience — like your language choice, theme, and recent addresses.

Cookie Purpose Duration
ic_lang Remembers your language choice 1 year
ic_theme Stores your light/dark mode preference 1 year
ic_recent Saves your recent pickup and drop-off locations 30 days

2.3 Analytics (recommended)

These cookies help us understand how people use InstantCab so we can improve it. Data is aggregated and anonymized.

Cookie Purpose Duration
_mixpanel Tracks app usage patterns 1 year
_amplitude Measures feature adoption 1 year
_sentry Captures crash reports Session

2.4 Marketing (opt-in)

These cookies track your visits across websites so we (or our partners) can show you relevant advertising. We only use these cookies if you explicitly opt in.

Cookie Purpose Duration
_fbp Facebook Pixel — measure ad conversions 90 days
_gcl Google Ads — track campaign performance 90 days
_ttclid TikTok Ads — attribution tracking 90 days

3 Third-party cookies

Some cookies are set by third parties we work with. These include:

  • Stripe — payment processing and fraud prevention
  • Mixpanel & Amplitude — product analytics
  • Google Maps — map display and location search
  • Sentry — error and crash tracking
  • Meta, Google, TikTok — advertising (only with consent)

Each of these providers has its own privacy policy. We encourage you to review them for full details.

4 Manage your preferences

You control which cookies InstantCab uses (except the strictly necessary ones). Changes take effect immediately.

Strictly necessary

Required for the app to work — sign-in, payments, security.

Functional

Remember your language, theme, and recent locations.

Analytics

Help us understand how the app is used (anonymized).

Marketing

Personalized ads on other platforms.

5 Browser controls

You can also control cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers let you:

  • See what cookies are stored on your device
  • Delete individual cookies or all of them
  • Block third-party cookies
  • Block all cookies (may break some sites)
  • Set "Do Not Track" preferences

Here are direct links to instructions for common browsers:

  • Google Chrome
  • Apple Safari
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Microsoft Edge
Important: If you disable strictly necessary cookies, some parts of InstantCab won't work — like staying signed in or completing a payment.
