Privacy Policy

How InstantCab collects, uses, and protects your personal information.

Last updated: March 15, 2025

Contents

1. Overview 2. What we collect 3. How we use it 4. Who we share with 5. Your rights 6. Security 7. Data retention 8. Children 9. Changes 10. Contact

1 Overview

InstantCab Technologies Inc. ("InstantCab", "we", "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have.

This policy applies to our mobile apps, website, and any other services we offer. It applies to both riders and drivers.

Our commitment: We collect only what we need to operate the service. We never sell your personal data. We give you control over what we collect and how it's used.

2 What we collect

We collect information you give us, information we get automatically, and information from third parties.

Category Examples Why
Account info Name, email, phone, profile photo To create and manage your account
Location data Pickup and drop-off, real-time GPS To match you with a driver and track trips
Payment info Card details (via Stripe), transaction history To process payments and issue receipts
Trip details Routes, times, distances, driver & rider ratings To improve routes, resolve disputes, and provide support
Device info Device model, OS, unique identifiers, IP address To ensure app security and prevent fraud
Communications Chats with drivers, support tickets, emails To provide support and resolve issues
Usage data Features you use, pages you visit, session duration To improve the app and diagnose issues

2.1 Information you provide

  • Registration details (name, email, phone, password)
  • Payment methods and billing address
  • Profile photo and preferences
  • Feedback, ratings, and support requests
  • Driver documents (license, insurance, registration)

2.2 Information collected automatically

  • Precise location (with your permission)
  • Device identifiers and IP address
  • App usage patterns and crash reports
  • Referral source and marketing campaigns

3 How we use your information

We use your personal information to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve our services
  • Match riders with drivers and calculate fares
  • Process payments and send receipts
  • Enable communication between riders and drivers
  • Provide customer support and resolve disputes
  • Ensure safety and prevent fraud
  • Send you important service updates
  • Send marketing (only with your consent — you can opt out anytime)
  • Comply with legal and regulatory requirements
Marketing: You control whether we send you promotional content. You can turn off marketing messages at any time in Settings → Notifications.

4 Who we share with

We never sell your personal data. We share it only as described below:

4.1 With drivers and riders

When you book a ride, your driver sees your first name, profile photo, pickup location, and any notes you've added. You see the driver's name, photo, vehicle, and rating. Phone numbers are masked through the app — neither party sees the other's real number.

4.2 With service providers

  • Payment processors (Stripe) — to charge your card
  • Cloud hosting (AWS) — to run our infrastructure
  • Analytics (Mixpanel) — to understand app usage
  • Communications (Twilio) — for SMS and voice calls
  • Support tools (Zendesk) — to answer your requests

All service providers are contractually required to protect your data and use it only for the services they provide to us.

4.3 With law enforcement

We may disclose information when legally required — for example, in response to a subpoena or court order — or when necessary to protect the safety of our users or the public.

4.4 In business transfers

If InstantCab is acquired or merges with another company, your information may be transferred. We'll notify you before that happens, and the new owner will remain bound by this Privacy Policy.

5 Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the following rights:

  • Access — request a copy of your personal data
  • Correction — fix inaccurate information
  • Deletion — ask us to delete your account and data
  • Portability — receive your data in a portable format
  • Restriction — limit how we use your data
  • Objection — opt out of marketing or certain processing
  • Withdraw consent — at any time, without penalty

To exercise any of these rights, email privacy@instantcab.com or use the controls in Settings → Privacy. We'll respond within 30 days.

California residents: You have additional rights under the CCPA, including the right to know what personal information we've collected and to opt out of any "sale" of your data. (We don't sell your data — but you still have the right to opt out.)

6 Security

We protect your information with industry-standard security measures:

  • 256-bit TLS encryption for all data in transit
  • AES-256 encryption at rest for sensitive data
  • PCI-DSS compliant payment processing
  • Regular security audits and penetration testing
  • Strict employee access controls and background checks
  • SOC 2 Type II certified infrastructure

No system is 100% secure. If we ever experience a data breach affecting your personal information, we'll notify you and the relevant authorities within 72 hours, as required by law.

7 Data retention

We keep your data only as long as we need it:

  • Active accounts — while your account is open
  • After deletion — most data deleted within 30 days
  • Trip history — retained for 7 years for tax and legal compliance
  • Payment records — retained per financial regulations
  • Fraud prevention — some data retained longer if needed for investigations

8 Children's privacy

InstantCab is not intended for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with their information, please contact us at privacy@instantcab.com and we'll delete it.

9 Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make material changes, we'll notify you by email or through the app at least 14 days before the changes take effect.

Your continued use of InstantCab after the changes take effect means you accept the updated policy.

10 Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your data, our Data Protection Officer is available to help.

Questions about your privacy?

Our Data Protection Officer responds within 5 business days. For urgent issues, use live chat.
