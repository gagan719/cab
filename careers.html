Our values

What we believe

Four principles guide every decision we make — from product to people.

Riders first

Every feature, every policy, every decision starts with the question: does this make the ride better for the person in the back seat?

Fair to drivers

We pay our drivers more than the industry average and give them real flexibility. A great ride requires a great driver.

Better cities

Fewer empty cars, more shared rides, and an all-electric fleet by 2030. Profit and planet aren't opposites.

Safety without compromise

Real-time tracking, background-checked drivers, and a 24/7 safety team. Never ship a feature that trades safety for speed.

Small teams, big impact

We organize in small autonomous pods that ship weekly. No bloated teams, no endless meetings.

Global by default

Every product decision considers our 120+ cities. Localization isn't an afterthought — it's the starting point.

Benefits

What we offer

Compensation and perks designed to help you do your best work — and have a life outside of it.

Remote-first culture

Work from anywhere in the US, Canada, or EU. We have offices in SF, NYC, and Berlin for those who prefer them.

Top-tier health coverage

100% covered medical, dental, and vision for you and your dependents. Including mental health support.

Equity for everyone

Every full-time employee gets stock options from day one. When we win, you win.

Unlimited PTO (with a minimum)

Take what you need — but we require at least 15 days off per year, so no one burns out.

16 weeks parental leave

Fully paid, for all parents, from day one. Plus a phased return-to-work program.

$2,500 learning budget

Annual budget for courses, conferences, books, or anything that helps you grow.
