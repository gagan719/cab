About InstantCab

We're making cities move better.

InstantCab is on a mission to make urban mobility safe, affordable, and sustainable — for riders, drivers, and the cities we serve.

Our mission

Faster rides. Fairer pay. A cleaner planet.

Since 2022, InstantCab has been connecting millions of riders with professional drivers across 120+ cities. We built our platform on a simple idea: treat drivers like partners, not contractors — and everyone wins.

By giving drivers 80% of every fare and investing in an all-electric fleet by 2030, we're proving that profit and purpose can coexist. That's what better urban mobility looks like.

Our story

From a garage in San Francisco to 18 countries.

InstantCab started in 2022 when two former rideshare engineers got tired of watching drivers struggle. They spent a weekend building a prototype that paid drivers 80% of every fare. The first ride happened a week later.

Three years on, we operate in 18 countries, employ 400+ people across three continents, and continue to be the only major platform that lets drivers keep the majority of their earnings.

What we believe

Our values

Five principles that guide every decision — from product to policy to people.

Riders first

Every feature, every policy, every decision starts with the question: does this make the ride better for the person in the back seat?

Fair to drivers

We pay our drivers more than the industry average and give them real flexibility. A great ride requires a great driver.

Better cities

Fewer empty cars, more shared rides, and an all-electric fleet by 2030. Profit and planet aren't opposites.

Safety without compromise

Real-time tracking, background-checked drivers, and a 24/7 safety team. Never ship a feature that trades safety for speed.

Small teams, big impact

We organize in small autonomous pods that ship weekly. No bloated teams, no endless meetings.

Global by default

Every product decision considers our 120+ cities. Localization isn't an afterthought — it's the starting point.
